Our Story

From a Small Pond to Nigeria's Premier Fish Farm

AquaHarvest Farms Ltd was founded in 2009 by Dr. Emmanuel Okafor with a simple vision: to make quality, affordable fish accessible to every Nigerian household while creating sustainable livelihoods for rural communities.

What began as a 2-hectare catfish farm in Sagamu, Ogun State has grown into a multi-state aquaculture enterprise with modern production facilities, a cold chain processing plant, and a nationwide distribution network.

Today, AquaHarvest is recognized as a leading force in Nigerian aquaculture — not just as a business, but as a movement that is transforming food security and empowering farming communities across Nigeria.
