What We Offer

Premium Aquaculture Services

We provide end-to-end solutions for the fish farming industry

🐟

Fish Production

Large-scale production of African catfish, Nile tilapia, and carp in modern ponds. We use sustainable methods to deliver fresh, healthy fish year-round.

🔬

Hatchery Services

Production of high-quality fingerlings and juveniles for farmers nationwide. Our hatchery operates with state-of-the-art equipment.

❄️

Fish Processing

Fresh, frozen, smoked, and dried fish products processed in our NAFDAC-certified cold chain facility for maximum quality.

🚚

Distribution & Supply

Reliable nationwide delivery network serving markets, restaurants, supermarkets, and exporters across West Africa.

📚

Training Programs

Professional aquaculture training for aspiring fish farmers. Programs include hands-on practical sessions and certification.

🤝

Consulting Services

Expert advisory for setting up and managing profitable fish farms. Site assessment, business planning, and ongoing support.

🥫

Fish Feed Supply

High-quality, nutrient-rich fish feeds for catfish, tilapia, and other species. Available in starter, grower, and finisher formulations.

🏗️

Pond Construction

Professional design and construction of earthen, concrete, and tarpaulin ponds. We handle everything from planning to completion.

🌐

Export Services

We facilitate fish product exports to neighboring African countries with full documentation and quality compliance support.
