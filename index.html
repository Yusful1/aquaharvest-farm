Who We Are

Pioneering Sustainable Aquaculture in Nigeria

Founded in 2009, AquaHarvest Farms Ltd has grown to become Nigeria's most trusted fish farming enterprise. We combine modern aquaculture technology with environmental sustainability to produce the finest fish products.

From our farms in Ogun, Lagos, and Kano States, we supply fresh, smoked, and frozen fish to households, restaurants, supermarkets, and exporters across West Africa.
