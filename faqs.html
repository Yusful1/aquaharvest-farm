FAQs

Got Questions? We Have Answers

🐟 General Questions

What species of fish does AquaHarvest produce? ▼
We primarily produce African Catfish, Nile Tilapia, and Common Carp. Our hatchery also produces fingerlings for sale.
Where are your farms located? ▼
Our main farm is in Sagamu, Ogun State. We also have branches in Lagos and Kano States.
Are your fish products NAFDAC approved? ▼
Yes! All AquaHarvest fish products are certified by NAFDAC and meet international food safety standards.
Do you use antibiotics or chemicals? ▼
No. AquaHarvest is committed to chemical-free, sustainable aquaculture without antibiotics or growth hormones.

🛒 Purchasing & Delivery

What is the minimum order quantity? ▼
Fresh fish – 10kg; Frozen fish – 50kg; Smoked fish – 5kg; Fingerlings – 500 pieces.
Do you deliver nationwide? ▼
Yes, we deliver nationwide using refrigerated trucks. Delivery takes 24–72 hours depending on location.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept bank transfers, cash, POS payments, and mobile money. Corporate accounts can pay via invoicing.

📚 Training & Consulting

How do I enroll in training programs? ▼
Call +234 803 456 7890 or email training@aquaharvestfarms.ng. Training runs monthly, in-person or online.
How much does the training cost? ▼
Fees range from ₦50,000 (3-day workshop) to ₦200,000 (2-week residential program).
Do you offer consulting for new farmers? ▼
Yes! We offer site assessment, pond design, business planning, and ongoing technical support.
