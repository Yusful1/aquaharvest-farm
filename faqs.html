FAQs

Got Questions? We Have Answers

🐟 General Questions

What species of fish does AquaHarvest Farm produce? ▼
We primarily produce African Catfish (Clarias gariepinus), Nile Tilapia, and Common Carp. Our hatchery also produces fingerlings of various species for sale to other fish farmers.
Where are your farms located? ▼
Our main farm and processing plant are in Sagamu, Ogun State. We also have branches in Lagos State (Badagry) and Kano State (Kumbotso), with distribution agents in 5 additional states across Nigeria.
Are your fish products NAFDAC approved? ▼
Yes! All AquaHarvest fish products are certified by NAFDAC and meet international food safety standards. We are also ISO 9001:2015 certified and hold the Good Aquaculture Practice (GAP) certification.
Do you use antibiotics or harmful chemicals in your fish farming? ▼
No. AquaHarvest is committed to chemical-free, sustainable aquaculture. We do not use antibiotics or growth hormones. Our fish are raised on certified natural feeds in clean, well-managed pond environments.

🛒 Purchasing & Delivery

What is the minimum order quantity? ▼
Minimum order quantities vary by product: Fresh fish – 10kg; Frozen fish – 50kg; Smoked fish – 5kg; Fingerlings – 500 pieces. We accommodate both small buyers and bulk corporate orders.
Do you deliver outside your base states? ▼
Yes, we deliver nationwide. We use refrigerated trucks for fresh and frozen fish deliveries. Delivery time is typically 24–72 hours depending on your location. Delivery fees apply based on distance and order size.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept bank transfers, cash payments at our farm gates, POS payments, and mobile money transfers. For large corporate orders, we offer invoicing with agreed payment terms.

📚 Training & Consulting

How do I enroll in your fish farming training program? ▼
You can enroll by visiting our contact page, calling our training line (+234 803 456 7890), or sending an email to training@aquaharvestfarms.ng. Training programs run monthly and can be attended in-person at our Sagamu or Lagos facility, or online via our virtual platform.
How much does the training cost and what does it cover? ▼
Training fees range from ₦50,000 (basic 3-day workshop) to ₦200,000 (comprehensive 2-week residential program). Topics covered include: pond construction, water quality management, fish feeding, disease management, harvesting, processing, and business management.
Do you offer consulting for new fish farmers? ▼
Absolutely! Our consulting services include site assessment, pond design, business planning, equipment procurement, and ongoing technical support. Contact us to schedule a free initial consultation with one of our aquaculture experts.

💼 Careers

How do I apply for a job at AquaHarvest Farms? ▼
Visit our Careers page to see current job openings. You can submit your application directly online. We also accept speculative applications via email at careers@aquaharvestfarms.ng. We encourage qualified candidates to apply, especially those with backgrounds in aquaculture, agriculture, food science, and business.
